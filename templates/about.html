About Iris Flower Explorer

Iris Flower Explorer is an interactive way to explore three Iris flower species: Setosa, Versicolor, and Virginica. Discover their characteristics, browse the dataset, compare their measurements, and use the Flower Finder to find the species that best matches your own measurements.

🌸

Three Species

Setosa, Versicolor, and Virginica each have their own look and typical measurements — get to know all three.

📊

Explore the Data

Browse the full dataset, filter it by species, and see how the measurements compare through charts.

🔎

Flower Finder

Type in the measurements of your own flower and instantly see which species it most resembles.
